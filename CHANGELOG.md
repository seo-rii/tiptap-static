## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-26

### Added

- Extension-driven sanitization without a runtime Tiptap dependency.
- Full, light, prepared hydration, protocol, and sanitizer-only entry points.
- Versioned direct hooks and descriptor runtimes for framework NodeViews.
- Integration coverage for real Tiptap 3, Svelte, React, and Vue components.
- Rendering performance and browser bundle-size comparison gates.
